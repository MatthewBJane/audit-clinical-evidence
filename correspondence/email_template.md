# Email to a journal: template

Sent from an @auditclinicalevidence.org address with two attachments, the
cover letter (`[ACE-ID]_letter.pdf`, from `letter_template.tex`) and the
report (`[ACE-ID].pdf`). Fill every bracket. Keep the body short: the letter
carries the summary and the report carries the detail. Record the send date
in `correspondence.yml` the same day.

To: [editor@example.org]
Cc: [integrity@example.org] (the publisher's research integrity office, where it has one)

---

**Subject:** Research integrity concern about [First author] et al. ([Year]), [Journal], DOI [10.xxxx/xxxxx] (ACE report [ACE-ID])

---

Dear [Recipient name],

Audit Clinical Evidence (ACE) is an independent initiative that publishes reproducible audits of clinical research. We write to make you aware of our audit of [Authors (Year)], "[Title]", published in [Journal] (DOI [10.xxxx/xxxxx]). The attached cover letter summarizes the findings and the attached report, [ACE-ID], gives them in full. The report is also published at [report URL] and permanently archived at https://doi.org/[report DOI].

In brief, [one or two sentences with the headline finding and what follows for the article's conclusions]. The report documents [N] errors or integrity concerns and recommends [retraction / a correction / an expression of concern]. The decision belongs to the journal.

We ask that the journal assess the report under its research integrity procedures and let us know the outcome. [ACE has not contacted the authors. / ACE wrote to the corresponding author on DATE and has received no reply.] We would welcome a response from the authors and will publish any response alongside the report, as described at https://auditclinicalevidence.org/disclaimers.

ACE records its correspondence with journals on a public page, https://auditclinicalevidence.org/correspondence, including the date of this message and of any reply. If this concern should go to a different office, please forward it or let us know the correct address.

Sincerely,

Matthew B. Jané
University of Connecticut
on behalf of Audit Clinical Evidence
https://auditclinicalevidence.org

Attachments: [ACE-ID]_letter.pdf, [ACE-ID].pdf
