Dear Professor Soares-Jr.,

Audit Clinical Evidence (ACE) is an independent initiative that publishes reproducible audits of clinical research. We write to make you aware of our audit of an article published in Clinics:

Abdelbasset, W. K., Alqahtani, B. A., Elshehawy, A. A., Tantawy, S. A., Elnegamy, T. E., & Kamel, D. M. (2019). Examining the impacts of 12 weeks of low to moderate-intensity aerobic exercise on depression status in patients with systolic congestive heart failure. A randomized controlled study. Clinics, 74, e1017. https://doi.org/10.6061/clinics/2019/e1017

The attached cover letter summarizes the findings. The attached report, ACE2026001, gives the full details and evidence supporting each error and concern. The report is also published at https://auditclinicalevidence.org/reports/ACE2026001 and permanently archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22861278.

In brief, the trial data in this article are duplicated across three published studies, the other two published in Medicine, and the reported PHQ-9 means and standard deviations could not have come from a real data set. Additionally, the three articles report different author contribution statements and three consecutive ethics approval numbers for what appears to be one trial. The report documents 29 errors or integrity concerns across the three articles and recommends retraction of the article. The decision belongs to the journal.

We ask that the journal assess the report under its research integrity procedures and the COPE guidelines, and let us know the outcome. ACE has not contacted the authors. We would welcome a response from the authors and will publish any response alongside the report, as described at https://auditclinicalevidence.org/disclaimers.

ACE records its correspondence with journals on a public page, https://auditclinicalevidence.org/correspondence, including the date of this message and of any reply. If this concern should go to a different office, please forward it or let us know the correct address.
