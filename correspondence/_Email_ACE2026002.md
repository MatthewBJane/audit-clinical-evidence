Dear Dr Siette,

Audit Clinical Evidence (ACE) is an independent initiative that publishes reproducible audits of clinical research. We write to make you aware of our audit of an article published in The Journals of Gerontology, Series A:

Singh, N. A., Clements, K. M., & Fiatarone, M. A. (1997). A randomized controlled trial of progressive resistance training in depressed elders. The Journals of Gerontology, Series A: Biological Sciences and Medical Sciences, 52A(1), M27-M35. https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/52A.1.M27

The attached cover letter summarizes the findings. The attached report, ACE2026002, gives the full details and evidence supporting each error. The report is also published at https://auditclinicalevidence.org/reports/ACE2026002 and permanently archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23331179.

In brief, every table in the article labels the values in parentheses as standard deviations, but they are standard errors. Meta-analysts who use the values as labeled inflate the trial's effect on depression by a factor of about four. The article is one of the most cited trials of exercise for depression, and 19 of the 24 meta-analyses we located that report an effect size for it assign a value larger than any possibly correct extraction, including a Cochrane review. The report documents 110 errors and recommends retraction of the article. The decision belongs to the journal.

We ask that the journal assess the report under its research integrity procedures and the COPE guidelines, and let us know the outcome. ACE has not contacted the authors. We would welcome a response from the authors and will publish any response alongside the report, as described at https://auditclinicalevidence.org/disclaimers.

ACE records its correspondence with journals on a public page, https://auditclinicalevidence.org/correspondence, including the date of this message and of any reply. If this should go to a different office, please forward it or let us know the correct address.
