**Subject:** Research integrity concern about two articles by Abdelbasset et al. (2019) in Medicine (ACE report ACE2026001)

---

Dear Editors,

Audit Clinical Evidence (ACE) is an independent initiative that publishes reproducible audits of clinical research. We write to make you aware of our audit of two articles published in Medicine:

Abdelbasset, W. K., & Alqahtani, B. A. (2019). A randomized controlled trial on the impact of moderate-intensity continuous aerobic exercise on the depression status of middle-aged patients with congestive heart failure. Medicine, 98(17), e15344. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000015344

Abdelbasset, W. K., Alqahtani, B. A., Alrawaili, S. M., Ahmed, A. S., Elnegamy, T. E., Ibrahim, A. A., & Soliman, G. S. (2019). Similar effects of low to moderate-intensity exercise program vs moderate-intensity continuous exercise program on depressive disorder in heart failure patients. Medicine, 98(32), e16820. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000016820

The attached cover letter summarizes the findings. The attached report, ACE2026001, gives the full details and evidence supporting each error and concern. The report is also published at https://auditclinicalevidence.org/reports/ACE2026001 and permanently archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22861278.

In brief, the trial data in these two articles are duplicated across three published studies, and the reported PHQ-9 means and standard deviations could not have come from a real data set. Additionally, in one of the papers the first author, Walid Kamal Abdelbasset, appears to have served as his own editor. The report documents 25 errors or integrity concerns in the two articles and recommends their retraction. The decision belongs to the journal.

Because Dr Abdelbasset is listed on the editorial board of Medicine, we have written to the editorial office and the publisher directly. We ask that the journal assess the report under its research integrity procedures and the COPE guidelines, and let us know the outcome. ACE has not contacted the authors. We would welcome a response from the authors and will publish any response alongside the report, as described at https://auditclinicalevidence.org/disclaimers.

ACE records its correspondence with journals on a public page, https://auditclinicalevidence.org/correspondence, including the date of this message and of any reply. If this concern should go to a different office, please forward it or let us know the correct address.
